About

TypingNonStop is a free typing practice app for people preparing for typing tests in government and office jobs, such as Junior Clerk, Data Entry Operator, Stenographer and court clerk posts.

What you can do

  • Practise with exam-style passages and see whether you reach the pass mark for that job.
  • See your net speed, which counts only correct words, because that is what exams judge.
  • Drill the words you miss most, race animal opponents, and learn touch typing from lesson 1.
  • Track your practice time, speed and accuracy, and climb ranks.

About the pass marks

Pass marks come from public job advertisements and notices (for example SSC, Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa public-sector adverts, the Election Commission of Pakistan, and US state and county civil service postings). They can change with every advertisement. Always read the official notice for the job you are applying to. This site is independent and is not connected to any government body.

Contact

Use the Send feedback button on the home screen of the app for questions, bug reports, passage requests or data requests.
